Powered by voice-core. The measuring runs in the real Rust library, compiled to WebAssembly, inside your browser. Nothing leaves your device. Pitch and pauses are checked against Praat on real speech clips. Pace has only been checked on synthetic bursts, so treat it as a rough guide. While you record, a live meter, speech flag and pitch come from voice-core's streaming analyzer. The steadiness tile (jitter, shimmer, HNR) lights up when voice-core gains it.
