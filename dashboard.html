How this is measured

Powered by voice-core. The measuring runs in the real Rust library, compiled to WebAssembly, inside your browser. Nothing leaves your device. Pitch and pauses are checked against Praat on real speech clips. Pace agrees with a Praat-based syllable detector on real clips, but there is no human syllable count, so treat it as a guide. While you record, a live meter, speech flag, pitch and pace come from voice-core's streaming analyzer. Jitter and shimmer (compared with Praat on the same real clips) and HNR are batch-only, so they appear once you stop or upload; they need a few seconds of voiced speech and read best on a sustained vowel. Not a medical test.
